Our team won the **Silver Prize (은상)** at the 2026 Industry-Academia Cooperation AI Hackathon, held August 19–20, 2026, under the theme *AI Agents for Work Automation*.

Our project, the **Privacy-Aware Contract Review Agent**, is an AI agent that detects and removes or pseudonymizes sensitive information in contracts before they are reviewed by external AI.
