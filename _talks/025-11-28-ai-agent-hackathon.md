I'm happy to share that our team has won the **Grand Prize (대상)** in the recent AI Agent Hackathon!

Our winning project, **Uni-SME**, is an innovative AI agent designed to bridge the gap between universities and Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs). Uni-SME connects students and SMEs by intelligently matching them based on their respective backgrounds, project requirements, and organizational goals.

We believe this platform will foster valuable collaboration, drive innovation, and create new opportunities for both groups.
